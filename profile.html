Profile

Avv. Luca Quartapelle

I provide advice and assistance, in and out of court, in civil matters and in criminal defence. Enrolled with the Ordine degli Avvocati (Bar Council) of Teramo.

On the civil side I handle compensation for damages and road traffic accidents, family and personal status, contracts, debt recovery and enforcement proceedings; on the criminal side I assist the person under investigation, the defendant and the victim of an offence at every stage of the proceedings. I also practise immigration law.

I accept instructions also outside the district: electronic court filing allows matters to be handled remotely and, where attendance at a hearing is required, a colleague of the court seised is appointed for service. I am enrolled on the lists for State-funded legal aid, in the civil and the criminal sector.

No outcome in court can be promised by any lawyer: the uncertainty of litigation cannot be removed, and it should be understood before deciding whether to face it.

Writing to this office

The case may be outlined by email or by telephone, in brief: the name of the opposing party, any time limits already running, and a short description. The preliminary examination entails no charge and no commitment: it serves to verify that there is no conflict of interest and to identify the time limits that are running. There is no need to be in Teramo to grant the retainer, since conference, mandate, correspondence and filings can all take place remotely; the conference may also be held by video call.
